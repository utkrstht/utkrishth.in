Iced Coffee
Ingredients:
• Half Tablespoon coffee
• Blender (Very important!!)
• 2 cup milk
• Chocolate syrup (Decoration)
• 2 tbsp sugar or sweetener
• 4 Ice cubes

Recipe:
1. In the blender, add your milk, coffee, sugar and blend.
2. In a glass (preferably tall for decoration), add circle layers of chocolate syrup.
3. Fill the glass with ice cubes.
4. Slowly pour the cold coffee over the ice.
5. Drink.
6. Wash glass.

French Fries
Ingredients:
• 3-4 large potatoes
• Oil for deep frying
• Salt to taste
• 30 gm cornflour
• 20 gm all purpose flour
• Pepper to taste
• Paprika

Method:
1. Wash and peel potatoes, cut into fry-shaped strips
2. In a bowl, add your fries, cornflour, the other flour, your spices and half of the salt and mix.
3. Heat oil to 175C in a deep fryer or large pot
4. Fry fries for 2 minutes, then remove
5. Fry fries again for 4 minutes or until golden and crispy. Then remove.
6. Sprinkle your remaining salt, on the fried fries.
7. Eat.

Soy Kebabs
Ingredients:
• 150 gm Soya Chunks
• 120 gm Chickpea flour
• 2 onions
• 3 green chillis
• Some ginger and garlic cloves (peeled, you monster)
• 1 tsp Black Pepper Powder, Dry Mango Powder, Cumin Powder, Red Chilli Powder, Turmeric Powder
• 3 tbsp Clarified Butter
• Stick (chopstick or something idk)

Method:
1. Put your soya chunks in a bowl, and add HOT (70-80C) water and move it aside for 15-20 minutes, meanwhile do step 2.
2. Chop your onions into 4 pieces, and add the chopped onions, ginger and garlic, green chillies and blend into a paste.
3. Once the soya chunks are soft, drain the water, rinse with cold water, then press it down to remove the excess water, and blend to shreds.
4. In a bowl, put the soya shreds in, clarified butter, all the spices, the paste, chickpea flour, and knee your dough.
5. Grab a handful of dough and mold it with your hand into a kebab shape, don't make very thin though
6. Fry on medium heat for 4-5 minutes
